Koiccha Ful

    Detail:

    It is not officially called koiccha ful. I actually don't know what it is called in other parts of my country (I'm no flower expert!). In English, it is called a water hyacinth flower. It is very, very common in Bangladesh, usually during the rainy season. It grows on pretty much every unregulated medium to big size body of water. You got a small pond by your house? Just make sure there is no major movement in the water, and it will grow there in a year or two! It is also common among teenage girls to use it to decorate their hair.
