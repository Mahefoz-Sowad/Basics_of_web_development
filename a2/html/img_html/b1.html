Sun Rise Near a Bhairab Railway Station

    Detail:

    I got lucky with the timing. It was around 2AM, I don't usually go out this late, especially not here, since it's by the Bhairab Municipal Area Cemetery near the Bhairab Railway Station. One of my teachers was moving out of town, and because his train was late at night, he asked me to come with him. A friend of mine and I went along to see him off. As we were walking back home after my teacher got on the train, I noticed the sky looking almost straight out of a dream. That is when I took this picture.
